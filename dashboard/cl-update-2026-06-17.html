Over 1,000 distinct CL pages earned clicks this period (up from ~500 the period before). Growth has shifted from a few hit titles to the long tail. Theo of Golden overtook Shield of Sparrows, which cooled from 1,759 → 979 clicks as its release buzz faded.

The takeaway

CL is a maturing organic acquisition surface — growing on breadth, not just hits.

Traffic is ~81% organic search, ~10% direct, ~7.5% paid, with a thin referral/email/social tail and a small (~0.3%) but worth-watching AI-assistant channel. Every source grew period-over-period: organic clicks +46%, product page views +22%.

CTR recovered to 4.21% with position flat — CL pages still aren't being suppressed the way the rest of the site's organic has been. And with 1,000+ pages now earning clicks, growth is coming from the long tail of the catalog, not a handful of new releases.

How to read these numbers

  • • The three sources don't cross-foot, by design. GSC clicks (711/day) count only direct landings on a CL URL. GA4 organic sessions (~1,684/day) count any session that touched a CL page, even if the user landed elsewhere first — so GA4 is legitimately larger. Amplitude counts deduplicated visitor views (UTC); GA4 counts pageviews (PT). Different bases, same upward direction.
  • • Data is reliable. The early-April 2026 GA4 tracking gap is well outside this window.
  • • GSC totals come from the date-level rollup (authoritative, uncapped); the per-page list capped at 1,000 rows, but the excluded tail is ~1 click each — immaterial.
  • • Page set: URLs containing /character-list/. Window: 28 days ending June 16, 2026 vs the prior 28 days.
